Visibility Into Student Thinking and Agency
Start with one task
What do your students need to think through themselves?
⬛ When the product is all there isA task you already teach, its learning goal, and any success criteria.
🟩 When reasoning is made visibleA plan for AI use and a way to check what students understand.
Step 1
Initial Audit
Describe the task, identify the thinking students need to do, and choose an initial AI-use level. Work independently before consulting AI.
Step 2
The Augmented Human Thinking Routine
Test the task with AI, check its output, and decide what to adjust before teaching. Allow about 30 minutes.
Step 3
Final Audit
After teaching, use student work and observations to decide what to keep or change.
Audit → Test and revise → Teach and collect evidence → Reflect. Save before leaving; your draft stays in this browser only.
