Privacy

Your notes, your choices

Start as a guest with notes saved in this browser. Optional accounts let you explicitly save copies to a private account. This page explains the difference.

What is stored, and where

Guest notes are saved in this browser’s local storage. They are not uploaded automatically. Signing in alone does not upload them. Choose “Save a new account copy” to send the current cycle to Supabase under your account. Opening an account copy adds a new local cycle without replacing existing local notes.

Because the data lives in your browser, it can be lost if you clear your browsing data, use a private window, or if the browser removes site data on its own. It also does not follow you to another device. Use My cycles → Download a backup to keep a copy, and import that file wherever you want to continue.

Optional accounts

Credentials are handled by Supabase Auth. Account cycle copies are held in the VISTA Supabase project and protected by account ownership policies. They can include your typed responses, exploration notes, annotations and reviewed imported text. They are not end-to-end encrypted from the service operator. There is no school administrator dashboard. Account copies do not sync automatically as you edit.

Signing out does not erase this browser’s local notes. On a shared device, local work may remain visible to the next person. Keep backups and use a private device for private work. Original name-only accounts remain supported but have no deliverable recovery email; new accounts use email.

Documents you import

If you import text from a PDF, Word, or text file, the file is read inside your browser. It is not uploaded, and the file itself is not kept. Only the text you review and accept is stored with your cycle.

Reports and backups

Reports and backup files are created on your device. Where they go after that is your decision.

What does leave your browser

  • Page-view analytics. The site uses Vercel Web Analytics. It records which pages are visited, the referring site, and approximate country, browser, operating system, and device type. It does not use cookies; visitors are counted with a hash that resets daily. It does not receive anything you type in the tool.
  • AI tools you open. The routine suggests prompts and offers links to AI tools. Those are separate services with their own terms. Anything you paste there is governed by them and by your school’s policies, not by VISTA.
  • Inquiry form. The form on the schools page composes an email in your own mail app. Nothing is sent until you send it.

Students

VISTA is for teachers. Do not enter student names or details that could identify an individual student, in the tool or in any AI tool you use alongside it. Describe patterns and anonymised examples.

School leaders

There is no administrator view. A school cannot see a teacher’s notes through VISTA. Teachers can choose to share a report or save an account copy. Account copies are private to their account through the application’s access policies; service administrators remain technically able to administer the underlying storage.

Changes

Local saving and account copies are distinct. Keep your own backup; this site does not currently provide a self-service account-deletion or account-data export dashboard. Local backups and reports remain available.
