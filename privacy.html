Privacy

Your work stays on your device

VISTA has no accounts and no database of teacher responses. This page describes exactly what is stored, where, and what leaves your browser.

What is stored, and where

Everything you type in the tool is saved in your browser’s local storage on the device you are using. It is not transmitted to VISTA, to a school, or to anyone else. We cannot see it, recover it, or delete it for you.

Because the data lives in your browser, it can be lost if you clear your browsing data, use a private window, or if the browser removes site data on its own. It also does not follow you to another device. Use My cycles → Download a backup to keep a copy, and import that file wherever you want to continue.

Documents you import

If you import text from a PDF, Word, or text file, the file is read inside your browser. It is not uploaded, and the file itself is not kept. Only the text you review and accept is stored with your cycle.

Reports and backups

Reports and backup files are created on your device. Where they go after that is your decision.

What does leave your browser

  • Page-view analytics. The site uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page visits without cookies and does not receive anything you type.
  • AI tools you open. The routine suggests prompts and offers links to AI tools. Those are separate services with their own terms. Anything you paste there is governed by them and by your school’s policies, not by VISTA.
  • Inquiry form. The form on the schools page composes an email in your own mail app. Nothing is sent until you send it.

Students

VISTA is for teachers. Do not enter student names or details that could identify an individual student, in the tool or in any AI tool you use alongside it. Describe patterns and anonymised examples.

School leaders

There is no administrator view. A school cannot see a teacher’s notes through VISTA. A teacher can choose to share a report; that is the only way their reflections reach anyone else.

Changes

If accounts or shared storage are ever added, they will be optional, described here first, and will not change how the account-free tool handles your data.
