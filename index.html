Remember · Navigate · Reimagine

Start with the teaching you value.

Remember

What made you proud to be a teacher? What were students doing, and why did it matter? Keep that purpose in view.

Navigate

Investigate a real uncertainty. Compare outputs, check sources, weigh consequences, and make a decision you can revisit.

Reimagine

Name one possibility worth pursuing, one condition it needs, and one first step. Invite a colleague to think with you.

A wider professional-learning journey

Five arcs. A record that can grow with you.

The arcs connect your relationship with AI, responsibility to students, learning design, classroom evidence, and professional and collective learning. They are areas for inquiry, not levels to pass or a score of your competence.

Within that journey, the six-stage assessment cycle helps you connect what you predicted, observed, decided, tried, and reconsidered.

Understand the method

Guidance when you need it.

Choose the essentials, one section at a time, or more explanation. Open an example, compare an original and revised task, or prepare questions for a real conversation.

Support is a choice you can change. Using less AI—or none—can be a well-justified outcome.
